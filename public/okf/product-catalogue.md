# Product Catalogue Categories

Thilaka Ayurveda organizes its catalogue across 12 broad wellness categories spanning [5 classical preparation forms](./preparation-forms.md). Individual product names, verified ingredients, pack sizes, and current counter availability are confirmed directly at the shop (see [Visiting Guidelines & FAQs](./visiting-and-faqs.md)).

## Categories by Wellness Group

### Daily & General Wellness
- **Respiratory Care** (`/products/respiratory-care/`): Products presented for respiratory wellness and immunity.
- **Immune Support** (`/products/immune-support/`): Herbal products presented for general immune support.
- **Oral & Dental Care** (`/products/oral-and-dental-care/`): Products presented for oral and dental care.

### Internal & Metabolic
- **Digestive Health** (`/products/digestive-health/`): Products presented for digestive and gut wellness.
- **Detox & Cleansing** (`/products/detox-and-cleansing/`): Products presented for cleansing and detox-related wellness.
- **Diabetes & Metabolic Health** (`/products/diabetes-and-metabolic-health/`): Products presented in relation to blood sugar and metabolic health.
- **Weight Management** (`/products/weight-management/`): Products presented for weight-management purposes.

### Mobility & Structural
- **Joint & Muscle Care** (`/products/joint-and-muscle-care/`): Products presented for joint and muscle wellness.
- **Bone & Spine Health** (`/products/bone-and-spine-health/`): Products presented for bone and spinal wellness.

### Personal & Life-Stage
- **Women's Health** (`/products/womens-health/`): Products presented for women's wellness.
- **Skin & Hair Care** (`/products/skin-and-hair-care/`): Products presented for skin and hair care.
- **Elderly Care** (`/products/elderly-care/`): Products presented for age-related wellness and vitality.
