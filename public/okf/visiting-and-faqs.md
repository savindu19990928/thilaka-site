## Frequently Asked Questions

1. **Where is the Thilaka Ayurveda shop located?**

2. **Can I purchase or order products through this website?**

3. **How can I check whether a specific product or raw herb is available?**
   Call `+94 36 226 6693` or prepare an availability enquiry via the Contact page before travelling.
4. **What are Thilaka Ayurveda's opening hours?**
   Please call `+94 36 226 6693` before visiting to confirm current opening days, hours, and Sri Lankan public or Poya holiday arrangements.
5. **What types of Ayurvedic product forms are available at Thilaka Ayurveda?**
   Churna, Vati / Gutika, Medicated Oils, Arishta / Asava, and fresh or dried Raw Herbs (see [Traditional Preparation Forms](./preparation-forms.md)).
6. **What is the difference between Churna, Vati / Gutika, Medicated Oils, and Arishta / Asava?**
   Churna are milled herbal powders; Vati / Gutika are tablets or pills; Medicated Oils are herbal oil infusions for specified applications; and Arishta / Asava are fermented herbal liquids.
7. **Does Thilaka Ayurveda offer personalized or custom herbal preparations?**
   Personalized preparations are described in our service scope; call `+94 36 226 6693` to confirm current availability and consultation requirements.
8. **Does Thilaka Ayurveda supply fresh and dried raw herbs?**
   Yes, subject to seasonal harvesting and shop stock. Call ahead to confirm specific botanicals and quantities.
9. **How can I verify ingredients, pack sizes, and usage directions before purchasing?**
   Inspect verified product labels at our Ruwanwella counter or call `+94 36 226 6693` for product-specific details.
10. **Does the website provide personal medical advice or diagnoses?**
    No. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional before using products for a health condition or alongside prescribed medication.
