# Thilaka Ayurveda — Business Profile & Services

**Thilaka Ayurveda** is a traditional Ayurvedic medicine shop and herbal-product retailer located at **No. 38, Main Street, Ruwanwella, Sri Lanka** (`+94 36 226 6693`). According to its official website, the business was established in **1982** and operates as a family business guided by traditional Ayurvedic knowledge and personal customer care.

## Core Values

1. **Tradition:** Respect for Ayurvedic knowledge, classical preparation forms, and heritage passed down since 1982.
2. **Care:** Helpful, unhurried, and respectful customer attention in store and by phone.
3. **Quality:** Attention to authentic herbal ingredients and accurate product communication.
4. **Transparency:** Clear communication about ingredients, [preparation forms](./preparation-forms.md), and counter availability.
5. **Responsibility:** Avoiding unsupported health claims and encouraging guidance from qualified healthcare professionals.

## Confirmed Service Scope

- **Traditional Ayurvedic Product Retail:** Customers inspect and purchase products in person at our Ruwanwella shop (see [Product Catalogue](./product-catalogue.md)).
- **Product Guidance & Availability Enquiries:** Call `+94 36 226 6693` or enquire before travelling to confirm product details and availability.
- **Personalized Herbal Preparations:** Subject to direct in-shop confirmation regarding formulation scope and practitioner requirements.
- **Fresh & Dried Raw Herbal Ingredients:** Seasonal supply subject to harvest and counter stock.
